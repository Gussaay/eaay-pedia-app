Paediatric recall questions - part 1 (typed by Dr A)

1- A 2 year old child presents with barking cough, hoarse voice and inspiratory stridor for 1 day. temp 37.8. What is the best initial treatment?
A) oral dexamethasone
B) IV ceftriaxone
C) nebulised salbutamol
D) chest x ray
E) intubation
Ans: A
Croup is treated with steroids, antibiotics have no role.

2- Which of the following is the commonest cause of nephrotic syndrome in children?
a) minimal change disease
b) membranous nephropathy
c) FSGS
d) post strep GN
answer = a
Minimal change disease accounts for the majority and responds to steroids.

3- A newborn of 36 weeks has bilious vomiting on day 1. abdominal xray shows double bubble. the most likely diagnosis:
A. duodenal atresia
B. pyloric stenosis
C. NEC
D. Hirschsprung
Correct: A
Double bubble = duodenal atresia, associated with Down syndrome.

5- a 5 year old with sickle cell disease has fever 39 and looks unwell. what is the most important immediate action?
A) blood cultures and IV antibiotics
B) reassure and give oral fluids
C) exchange transfusion
D) urgent MRI brain
E) vitamin D
Ans: A
Children with sickle cell are functionally asplenic. Sepsis can kill quickly.

6- Which vaccine is contraindicated in a child on high dose steroids?
A) MMR
B) inactivated polio
C) hepatitis B
D) DTP
Ans: A
Live vaccines are avoided in significant immunosuppression.
